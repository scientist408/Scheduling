Production Employee Scheduler

B1–B13 • Rework • Skill-Based Assignment • R/OFF • Other-Plant Allocation • Call-Off Replacement • 5/6/7-Day Schedule

1. Paste Employee List

Paste Employee Name and Employee ID from Excel. After loading, enter each worker’s skills. Use ALL for all machines and Rework, or enter items such as B1,B2,B5,REWORK.

2. Schedule Settings

The scheduler prioritizes any employee who has worked only one consecutive day, so the employee receives a second continuous workday before being assigned OFF whenever staffing and skill coverage allow it. Any remaining violations are shown in validation.

4. Allocate Employees to Other Plants

Select a worker, the first day at the other plant, and the number of days. Those days show as OTHER PLANT and the worker is excluded from this plant’s schedule.

5. Requested Off & Weekly Schedule

Before generating: click a day cell to mark R/OFF. After generating: click an assignment to change it to R/OFF, then click Rebalance Schedule. OTHER PLANT days cannot be changed from this table.

6. Manual Skill-Based Swap

Swap two workers between machines or Rework on the same day. The swap is blocked when either worker is not qualified, unless Manual Override is checked.
